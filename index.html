How it works

Documents

  1. Press Documents, then use the live camera or choose a file from your device.
  2. The page corners are found automatically. Drag the dots if they are off.
  3. Press Scan and clean. The page is straightened, shadows and ruled lines are removed, and the text is read.
  4. Check the text next to the scan. Lists and tables are kept in rows and columns.
  5. Export to Word, Excel, PowerPoint or PDF. Pick a format under “Export directly as” to download in one tap. Word and PowerPoint keep the page looking exactly like the original by default.
  6. Press Clear scan when you are done, or tick the box to clear after every export.

Photos

  1. Press Photos, then use the live camera or choose a photo.
  2. Turn on Black and white if you want it.
  3. Drag Enhance to sharpen a soft photo.
  4. Pick a motion style and press Play to animate it. Record saves it as a video.
  5. Save as JPEG, PNG or WebP, or export a PDF.

Tips: use good light and a flat page. Printed text reads best. Handwriting and blurry photos need a quick check before you export. Enhance sharpens what is there; it cannot restore detail that was never captured.
